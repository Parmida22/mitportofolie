About me:

Who is Parmida?

My family is scattered across different parts of the world, which means that Denmark is not the only place I consider home. I have two siblings: one younger brother and one older brother. My younger brother lives in Tehran with my mother, while my older brother lives in Zimbabwe with my father

My Journey

As mentioned earlier, I grew up in Farum, where I attended primary school. After finishing primary school, I attended Birkerød Gymnasium. After graduating, I took three gap years, each of which gave me very different experiences.

First gap year

During my first gap year, I worked full-time at McDonald's in Farum as a barista. This was also my first experience living on my own, which taught me a lot about independence and responsibility.

Second gap year

During my second gap year, I moved to Mozambique, where I began gaining work experience at my brother's logistics company Cross Country Containers

Third gap year

During my third gap year, I became more established in my role at the company. As my responsibilities increased, I moved from Mozambique to Zimbabwe. During this time, I also spent a lot of time thinking about my future and what I wanted to study. I eventually decided to pursue an education in IT Architecture at EK, which I am currently studying until October 2029.
